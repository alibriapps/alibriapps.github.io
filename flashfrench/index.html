FlashFrench

Flashcards for English speakers, with French audio on every card. Say or type your answer and find out straight away if you were right. Works offline, with no account and no ads.

How it works

  1. See a word. French to English, or English to French when you want more of a challenge.
  2. Answer. Type it, or tap the mic and say it out loud. Turn on Auto Mic for a hands-free session.
  3. Hear it. Every card has French audio, and you'll find out straight away if you were right. Missed a word? You'll see the correct spelling, with accents in the right place, before you move on.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Level A1 is free, with no time limit and no ads. You only pay if you want A2, B1 and B2.

Is FlashFrench Pro a subscription?

No. It's a one-off purchase. Pay once and the extra levels are yours.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for, and no ads or tracking.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for studying. Cards, French audio, typed answers and stats all work offline. Spoken answers use your phone's speech recognition, which may need a connection on some phones, and buying FlashFrench Pro needs a connection.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes. It's on the App Store now: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6825074299

Is it on Android?

Yes. FlashFrench is on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brianflynn.flashfrench

Will it get me through TCF IRN, TEF IRN, DELF or GCSE?

FlashFrench is a vocabulary app. It helps you learn the words at the CEFR levels those tests use, but it isn't a test course and it doesn't teach the test format, grammar or writing. There are no promised results. Use it alongside a course or proper test practice.
